voice lab

Audition a voice with the exact delivery direction the episode will use, on whichever provider TTS_PROVIDER points at. Click a voice to hear it, or click one of its style chips to hear that style. Cast as host / guest saves that voice together with the direction box next to it, and the studio uses it from the next line it renders — no restart. Azure Speech directions are directives (style=excited;degree=1.7;rate=+10%;pitch=+2st); OpenAI ones are plain prose. The casting lives in .codecast/cast.json; reset to .env throws it away. back to the studio
